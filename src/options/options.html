4. ATS formatting checklist

The match score on the application panel checks keywords only. These are the formatting mistakes that get an otherwise-strong resume rejected before a human sees it — worth a final pass on the file itself.

  • Save as a text-based .pdf or .docx — never a scanned image, .pages, or .odt.
  • Single column, no tables, no text boxes, no skill bars or graphics — ATS parsers read those as garbage or skip them.
  • Contact info in the body of the page, not a header or footer — many parsers never read those.
  • Standard section names: Experience, Education, Skills — not "My Journey" or "What I Bring".
  • Use the exact terms from the job description at least once (e.g. "Project Management", not just "led projects").
  • A keyword that matters should appear 2–4 times across the resume — more than that reads as stuffing.
